# Refactoring: The Key to Clean Code and Scalable Systems

Refactoring is an important part of software development that optimizes the code's internal structure without changing how the application works on the outside. For companies utilizing [event-driven architectures](https://www.confluent.io/learn/event-driven-architecture/) like [Apache Kafka®](https://www.confluent.io/what-is-apache-kafka/), refactoring becomes a strategic step towards achieving clean code, maintaining low [technical debt](https://www.confluent.io/learn/tech-debt/), and preparing the system for long-term growth.

By optimizing the structure of your code, refactoring helps keep your software and its fundamental architecture strong and flexible, making it better equipped to handle new challenges as they come up.
