Why titanium

## Strong as steel, half the weight, and it does not rust

That combination is why titanium ends up in aircraft, race bikes and implants — and why it is worth machining despite the cost.

### Strength to weight

Grade 5 reaches around 900 MPa tensile strength at roughly 56% of the density of steel. Same job, far less mass.

### Corrosion resistance

A self-repairing oxide layer means seawater, chlorides and most acids leave it alone — no plating, no galvanic surprises.

### Biocompatible

Grade 23 ELI is the standard choice for implants because the body tolerates it and it holds up under cyclic load.

Applications

## More on where titanium parts go

### Petroleum & offshore

Due to their weight and flexibility, titanium parts are widely applied in the petroleum and engineering industries. If you are committed to producing pipes, water management systems, or other deep-water units, this metal is an ideal choice. In addition to withstanding heavy loads, titanium cannot be affected by salt water. That is why it is common for making various extraction equipment, propeller shafts, valves, and fittings.

### Aerospace

Titanium is also known for its substantial resistance to high temperatures, and that turns it into the most preferred metal in aerospace applications. With the advent of jet engines, its role has become even more crucial — for those engaged in this industry, it is of key importance to ensure the safety of their products.

![MRI scanner in a hospital](https://titan-bolts.com/wp-content/plugins/a3-lazy-load/assets/images/lazy_placeholder.gif) ![MRI scanner in a hospital](https://titan-bolts.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/qty_2-235x300.jpg)

### Medical & dental

As a titanium screw manufacturer that holds leading positions on the market, Baoji Sino-Swiss Titanium Co., Ltd. works closely together with many healthcare facilities. With its non-toxic, non-ferromagnetic, and corrosion-resistant properties, titanium is a medical and dental choice for implants — it withstands bodily fluids for years, and a person with an implant can still be examined by MRI because titanium is not ferromagnetic.

### Automotive & motorsport

It is hardly possible to imagine the automotive industry without the use of racing lug bolts made of titanium. While improving the durability and weight distribution of a vehicle, the metal brings overwhelming advantages for the performance of bikes and cars. From connecting rods to camshafts, rocker arms, springs, and valves – titanium parts are the most popular when designing a powerful vehicle. With the aim to increase longevity and reduce weight, titanium exhaust systems have also been added to our product range.
